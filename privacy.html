Drive Agent privacy policy

Drive Agent runs only on my own computer. It reads files from Google Drive with the permission of the signed in account and copies them into my own Google Drive.

No data is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with anyone. Sign in tokens are stored encrypted on my computer only.

You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
